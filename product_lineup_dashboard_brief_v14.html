Counterfactual Product Lineup Model

Finance / Product leadership screen-share brief
Business question
If we redesign the lineup,
where do customers go?
1Estimate a latent mixture dynamic discrete choice model of how beliefs, complexity, and current product features drive sorting.
2Recover structural demand parameters from WTP price variation.
3Simulate TY26 lineup scenarios and predict conversion across new options.
No start Drop Finish in TY26 option
Executive takeaway

The model separates what customers believe, what complexity they actually have, and how they value product features. This lets us compare TY26 lineup scenarios on conversion, migration, and revenue instead of relying on current product shares.

Scenario move

Keep estimated customer states and preferences fixed. Replace menu attributes, prices, add on entitlements, and optional add ons. Recompute probabilities.

Scenario product share = start probability × completion probability, summed over beliefs, complexity, and optional add on bundles.
